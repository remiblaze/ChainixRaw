# Chainix Raw: one-knob FX chain

![Chainix Raw free one-knob FX chain UI](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RemiBlaze/ChainixRaw/main/chainixraw-ui-screenshot.png)

**One knob runs a whole FX rack. Turn it up and your sound gets bigger.**

Chainix Raw is the free, one-knob version of the multi-FX plugin **Chainix**. The single **CHAIN** macro drives a fixed, level-matched rack, saturation, glue compression, stereo width, delay, and reverb space, all scaled together so it just gets bigger, never a volume jump.

**macOS** (Apple Silicon and Intel): AU, VST3, CLAP, AAX, Standalone. Signed and notarized by Apple.

**Windows** 10 and 11, 64-bit: VST3, CLAP, Standalone. Authenticode signed.

AAX ships on macOS only.

---

## 🚀 Download & Install

Go to the [latest release](https://github.com/RemiBlaze/ChainixRaw/releases/latest) and pick your platform.

**macOS**
1. Download **`ChainixRaw_Installer.pkg`**.
2. Double-click it and follow the installer. It is signed and notarized by Apple, so it installs cleanly with no security warnings.
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins. Chainix Raw appears under **Remi Blaze**.

**Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit**
1. Download **`ChainixRaw_Installer.exe`**.
2. Run it and follow the installer. It is Authenticode signed.
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins. Chainix Raw appears under **Remi Blaze**.

No dongle and no extra account on either platform.

Full guide: **[remiblaze.com/support](https://remiblaze.com/support/)**.

---

## 🎛️ What It Does
Turn the one **CHAIN** knob. It runs your signal through a fixed, always-on rack, warm saturation, glue compression, stereo chorus width, a slap delay, and reverb space, all scaled together and level-matched, front-loaded so the first quarter of the knob is just a taste. At zero it's a clean bypass; open it up and the sound gets bigger and thicker. Works on drums, synths, vocals, and full mixes. Zero added latency.
